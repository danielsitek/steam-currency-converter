# Chrome Web Store listing draft

## Name and purpose

**Name:** Steam Currency Converter

**Single purpose:** Show approximate Steam Store prices in a currency selected by the user.

**Short description:** See approximate Steam Store prices in your chosen currency while browsing public pages.

**Detailed description:**

Steam Currency Converter shows estimated prices in your chosen currency on public Steam Store pages. Choose a currency from the extension popup, then browse the Store normally. Hover over a converted price to see its original amount and the date of the exchange rate.

Exchange rates come from Frankfurter and are cached for 12 hours. If the service is temporarily unavailable, the extension can use a previously cached rate. Converted values are estimates; Steam and your bank may charge or convert at a different rate.

The extension works on public Store pages. It does not change prices in the cart, checkout, account, sign-in, or purchase pages. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve or Steam.

## Privacy tab answers to verify before submission

- **Storage permission:** Saves the selected display currency and caches exchange rates.
- **Host permission for `api.frankfurter.dev`:** Fetches exchange rates for the source and target currency codes.
- **Steam Store page access:** Reads visible price text and accessibility labels locally to show converted estimates. It does not send page content or URLs to the developer or the rate service.
- **Remote code:** None. The remote API returns rate data; all executable code is bundled with the extension.
- **Privacy policy:** Publish and link to [`docs/privacy.md`](privacy.md). Verify that the public URL is reachable without signing in.
